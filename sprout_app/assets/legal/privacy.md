# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stackmint (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles personal information for our apps and services. It currently covers **Sprout**. A public copy is intended at privacy.stackmint.app; until that site is live, the version in the app is the current policy.

This document is written in plain language. It is not legal advice.

## Who we are

Stackmint is a trading name based in the Republic of South Africa. We are the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) for the personal information described here.

Contact: hello@stackmint.app

## What we collect

We collect:

- Account identifiers, such as your email and display name (including a name from Google if you sign in with Google)
- Content you enter in Sprout, such as savings accounts, goals, and transactions
- Technical data our processors need to run sign-in and optional cloud sync (for example an IP address at their network edge)

We do **not** collect your device location. We do **not** store your card numbers. Store purchases (when Premium is enabled) are handled by the app store and RevenueCat.

## Why we use it

We use this information to:

- Provide the app
- Authenticate you (email code or Google)
- Sync your data across devices when you are signed in
- Offer optional Premium features

We do not sell your personal information.

## Who we share with

We use service providers (operators) to run the service. They may include:

- Supabase (authentication and cloud sync)
- Google (sign-in, and Firebase Remote Config for in-app legal text and flags)
- RevenueCat (Premium, when that feature is enabled)

Google’s own privacy policy applies to Google sign-in. We do not share your savings records with other users.

## Where it is processed

Our processors may handle information outside South Africa. We remain the responsible party under POPIA.

## How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists, and as needed to operate the app. You can delete your account in the app. Deletion removes your savings data from this device and the cloud. You can also email hello@stackmint.app.

If you subscribe to Premium, deleting your account does not cancel store billing. Manage or cancel Premium in the store.

## Your rights

Under POPIA you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact us at hello@stackmint.app, or use in-app account deletion.

You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) if you believe we have not handled your information lawfully.

## Children

Sprout is for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly create accounts for children.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy in the app (including via Remote Config, without a store update). The current version is shown in the app. Continued use after an update means you accept the new policy.
